Case 04 — SaaS · Web

Lumen Analytics — data that makes sense.

A Next.js analytics dashboard for a fintech challenger — custom charting, real-time sync and an interface people actually enjoy reading numbers in.

Year
2024
Scope
Product design · Dashboard build
Stack
Next.js, TypeScript, WebSockets

The challenge

Lumen’s customers were exporting data to spreadsheets because the old dashboard buried answers under filters and tabs. Activation stalled: new users couldn’t reach their first insight fast enough to stay.

What we built

  • A rebuilt information architecture centred on questions, not tables
  • Custom chart components tuned for financial time-series data
  • Real-time sync over WebSockets with optimistic UI updates
  • Role-based saved views so teams share one source of truth
  • Performance work that holds a 98 Lighthouse score in production

The outcome

Time-to-first-insight dropped 71% and activation rose 34% within two release cycles. Spreadsheet exports — the old escape hatch — fell by more than half as teams moved their reporting into Lumen itself.
